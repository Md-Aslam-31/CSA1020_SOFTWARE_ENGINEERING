# CampusCart — one-week sprint plan

## Sprint goal

Let a campus member find an active marketplace listing and let a seller publish a basic listing.

## Candidate sprint backlog

The four Sprint 1 stories total 18 points: CC-01 (5), CC-02 (5), CC-03 (3), and CC-04 (5). CC-03 depends on CC-02. CC-04 depends on CC-01. The team should confirm its own velocity before committing; the point total is a planning estimate, not a promise.

## Sprint events

- Planning: agree on the goal, capacity, and definition of done.
- Daily check-in: share progress and blockers; update Jira status truthfully.
- Review: demonstrate sign-in, listing search/detail, and listing creation that actually work.
- Retrospective: record one practice to keep and one change for the next sprint.

## Definition of done

Acceptance criteria pass; the change is reviewed; no known blocker prevents the demonstrated flow; and Jira status reflects the real state. No completion is claimed until the Jira account is connected and the learner performs the work.
