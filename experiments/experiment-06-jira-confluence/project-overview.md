This local outline mirrors the structure of the published page. The live account page is [here](https://sofenglab.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CAMPUSLIB/pages/5603329/Library+Management+System+Project+Overview).

## Objective

Help students locate, reserve, and collect campus-library books, and help librarians monitor circulation and availability.
